# Playground

Your browser’s User-Agent is loaded automatically. Choose a sample or enter another User-Agent. Detection runs locally in your browser; the string is not sent anywhere.

<DevicePlayground />

This demo adds `isRawClient` (curl or wget) and `isCrawler` (Googlebot only). You can edit the User-Agent field manually: for example, enter `wget/1.21.4` to see `isRawClient` become `true`.

These are example checks, not a full bot catalog. The playground does not supply CDN headers.
